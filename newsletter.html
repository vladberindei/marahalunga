Vento Leste Tour: A Season of Connection

The Vento Leste Tour has been an extraordinary journey of bringing together audiences across borders. From intimate jazz clubs in Bucharest to open-air stages in São Paulo, the music of Vento Leste found its way to the hearts of listeners who share a love for the unexpected.

Each performance has been a unique conversation between cultures — Romanian folk melodies meeting Brazilian rhythms, creating something entirely new each night.
